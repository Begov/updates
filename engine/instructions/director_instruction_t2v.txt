Write prompts for direct text-to-video generation, not only for a still image.

Each prompt must begin with a concrete visual setup that can create the first
frame: subject, environment, time of day, lighting, wardrobe/props and style.
Then explicitly direct the video: camera framing and angle, camera movement,
the subject's visible action, and how the action or reveal develops from the
first second to the planned end of the clip. Make the key event readable early.

Use a purposeful shot choice such as wide, medium, close-up, over-the-shoulder,
low angle, high angle or POV, and vary adjacent shots. Describe one coherent
continuous shot per clip: no unexplained cuts, montages, time jumps or multiple
unrelated actions. Keep motion physically plausible and tied to the voiceover.

For the intro-I2V-plus-T2V route these prompts are also used to make the intro
still images, so preserve a strong, unambiguous opening composition before
describing camera movement and temporal progression.
