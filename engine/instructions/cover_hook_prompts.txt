ОЖИВЛЕНИЕ ОБЛОЖКИ — первый кадр ролика

Зритель кликнул на обложку и сразу видит её же, только ожившую. Кадр, который
он выбрал глазами, продолжается в движении — это держит первые секунды.

Промпт выбирается настройкой «Обложка: режим» рядом с интро-блоком. Правьте
тексты под себя: файл не перезаписывается обновлениями.

Главное правило для всех режимов: обложка должна ОСТАТЬСЯ УЗНАВАЕМОЙ. Зритель
только что видел её в ленте — если картинка уедет в сторону или сменит вид,
эффект узнавания пропадёт, а вместе с ним и смысл затеи.

--------------------------------------------------------
ПРОМПТ 1 — НАЕЗД (zoom_in)
--------------------------------------------------------

A slow cinematic push-in on this exact image: the camera creeps forward,
the frame tightens by a few percent over the shot. Everything stays in place —
same composition, same subject, same lighting, same colour grade and grain.
The move is gentle and continuous, no easing at the end, no cut, no shake.
Photoreal, identical to the source frame.


--------------------------------------------------------
ПРОМПТ 2 — ОТЪЕЗД (zoom_out)
--------------------------------------------------------

A slow cinematic pull-back from this exact image: the camera drifts backwards,
revealing a little more of the scene at the edges. The subject keeps its place
in the frame and stays fully visible the whole time. Same composition, lighting,
colour grade and grain as the source. Smooth continuous move, no cut, no shake.
Photoreal.


--------------------------------------------------------
ПРОМПТ 3 — ДРЕЙФ (drift)
--------------------------------------------------------

The camera glides slowly sideways past this exact scene, giving it real depth:
foreground shifts a touch faster than background. The photographed content
itself is frozen — nobody moves, nothing changes shape or place. Framing stays
centred on the same subject. Same lighting, colour grade and grain as the
source. Photoreal, one steady move, no cut.


--------------------------------------------------------
ПРОМПТ 4 — ЗАЛЁТ В СЦЕНУ (fly_in)
--------------------------------------------------------

The camera flies forward INTO this exact scene, travelling through its depth
past the foreground toward the main subject, as if entering the picture.
The world inside is static geometry — nothing moves on its own. The subject
stays centred and recognisable throughout. Same lighting, colour grade and
grain as the source. Photoreal, one continuous move, no cut.


--------------------------------------------------------
ПРОМПТ 5 — ОЖИВАНИЕ (awaken)
--------------------------------------------------------

This exact frame comes alive: what would naturally move begins to move —
hair and fabric stir, steam and dust drift, water ripples, light flickers,
eyes blink. The camera itself barely moves. Composition, subject, pose,
lighting and colour grade stay exactly as in the source: the picture must
still read as the same image, only breathing. Photoreal, subtle, no cut.
