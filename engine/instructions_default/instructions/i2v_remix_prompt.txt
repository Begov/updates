Change the camera position. Choose a different angle. Reshoot the scene from a greater distance or at a 45–90 degree angle to the subject.
Any person facing directly toward the camera must be repositioned:
show them in side profile, three-quarter view, or from behind —
no direct frontal faces visible to the viewer.
CRITICAL: If the scene contains any children or minors (people who appear to be under 18),
they must be shown strictly from behind — facing away from the camera,
their face must not be visible at all, no exceptions.
If the scene contains destruction, debris, injury or physical distress,
pull the camera back to show a wider environmental context
rather than close detail of the damage.
Preserve the same lighting, color palette, time of day, and mood.
Do not add or remove people or objects.
